Books Every Software Engineer Should Read

These books are required reading for software engineers. Even if you did classes in computer science, you may not have heard of or read these. They cover topics pertinent to the practice of writing great software on schedule in a healthy culture.

Writing code

  • Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship by Robert C. Martin
  • Clean Architecture: A Craftsman's Guide to Software Structure and Design by Robert C. Martin
  • Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software by by Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, John Vlissides

Project Management

  • The Mythical Man-Month by Fred Brooks

Product Management

  • Inspired: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love by Marty Cagan
  • Empowered: Ordinary People, Extraordinary Products by Marty Cagan and Chris Jones

Culture

  • Rework by Jason Fried, David Heinemeier Hansson
  • It Doesn't Have to Be Crazy at Work: A Practical Guide to Sustainable Productivity Through Calm Company Culture and Effective Leadership by Jason Fried, David Heinemeier Hansson
  • Remote: Office Not Required by David Heinemeier Hansson, Jason Fried

Sales, Marketing, and Motivation

  • To Sell Is Human: The Surprising Truth About Moving Others by Daniel Pink
  • Drive: The Surprising Truth About What Motivates Us by Daniel Pink
  • Start with Why: How Great Leaders Inspire Everyone to Take Action by Simon Sinek
